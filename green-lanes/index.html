      How to use it

      1. Explore the riding areas and ride a ready-made route or trip.
      2. Plan your own: a Loop from a start, Draw roughly where you want to go, a multi-day Tour, or Find lanes.
      3. Change it: tap a green lane to add it, tap a pink one to take it out, or drag ○ onto a lane. To stop somewhere, tap its icon and choose Add to route, or drag ○ onto the icon.
      4. Show (under the search box) puts tea, food, toilets, fuel, beauty spots and places to stay on the map and in the GPX.
      5. GPX sends the route to your sat nav or phone app. Saved keeps what you have open and what you save.

      On the normal map: green is a byway, open to motorbikes; amber is an unclassified road, usually open; dashed green is a byway from the council's record; red dots are closed; grey dots are restricted byways, with no motorbikes. Satellite and Topo use their own colours, shown in the Map key.

      Before you ride: lanes close for repairs and for the winter. Check each lane on the TRF Green Road Map or TrailWise, and obey signs on the ground.

      How it works, where the data comes from, and how complete it is

      Lanes, stops and places come from OpenStreetMap, which volunteers keep up to date, so it has gaps. Roads come from the free BRouter and OSRM servers, which ask users to keep requests modest, so planning takes a few seconds.
